# Roadmap

## Next
- Finish a race on a script: a lap's worth of `--key` steering.
- Full screen. In software mode the game's full screen is the desktop
  switched to 640x480 (`autoFullScreen=1` asks for it at race start), which
  the host refuses. A borderless window scaled by the presenter would give
  the look without the mode change.
- `Setup.cmd` Quick start (REPO_RULES section 5), the same steps as the README's Step by step.
- A conformance harness: milestones (intro, title menu, race start) checked from a headless run.

## Later
- Hardware renderers: `TRID3D.DLL` natively, or a host Direct3D.
- Monster Truck Madness 1 (1996): unpack its ACME cabinets first.

## Out of scope
- Shipping any part of either game, or anything lifted from it.
